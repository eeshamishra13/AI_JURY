Refund Policy

Customers can request a full refund if they cancel their booking at least 48 hours before the scheduled service.

Cancellations made between 24 and 48 hours before the scheduled service are eligible for a 50% refund.

Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled service are not eligible for a refund.

Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.

No processing fee is charged for eligible refunds.
